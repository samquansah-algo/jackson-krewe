2 · The problem

Prior schooling gets lost at the registrar's desk.

Records nobody can read

Transcripts arrive in Spanish, Portuguese, Haitian Creole or Dari. Staff rebuild them by hand, or skip them.

Classes taken twice

Without credit, students repeat what they passed. Placing older students below grade is linked to dropping out.

A clock running out

Only 15% of the 2024 newcomer cohort left high school proficient in English. Many age out first.

Sources: Mantil et al. (2026); Jimerson (2001), meta-analysis of grade retention (retained students 2–11× more likely to drop out).

Context (not in the 60-second script): the dropout link comes from grade-retention research, which we apply by analogy to placing older newcomers below grade.
