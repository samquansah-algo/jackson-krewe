6 · What we learned

A transcript alone doesn't tell the whole story.

A teacher told us students with the same years of school arrive far apart: some lack basic number sense, while others are ready to leave ESL within months.

So every record gets a short diagnostic, run in school with an adult.

We also heard: self-paced tools go unfinished when students work alone at home.

Source: Mom Test interview with a South Carolina ESOL and math educator (2026). One interview; Massachusetts interviews are next.
