How it works, from enrollment to day 30

1

Enroll

2

3

Profile

Ready minutes after the check ends.

4

Decide

5

Inform

Family gets a text in their language, same day.

6

Review

Teacher confirms or flags at day 30.

7

Improve

Director sees placement across schools.

Every step happens during the school day. Nothing depends on homework.
