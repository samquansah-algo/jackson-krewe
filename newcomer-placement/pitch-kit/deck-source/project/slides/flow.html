How it works, from photo to credit

1

Upload

Family or caseworker sends photos by phone or WhatsApp.

2

Read

AI extracts and translates every course and grade.

3

Map

Courses matched to state requirements and credits.

4

Check

30-minute diagnostic in school, with an adult.

5

Sign off

Trained reviewer approves within 48 hours.

6

Decide

Registrar decides; family gets a summary in their language.

Every recommendation makes the course map smarter for the next student from the same system.

Appendix. The map of foreign courses to state credits improves with every reviewed transcript; that is the long-term advantage.
