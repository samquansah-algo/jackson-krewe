Business Model Canvas

Key partners

MA DESE language office; pilot districts; bilingual educators; university researchers; EL data platforms

Key activities

Build and validate items; record audio; run pilots; train staff

Value proposition

See each newcomer's math and home-language starting point on day one. Consistent, defensible placement. Families understand why.

Relationships

Co-designed pilots; in-person training; quarterly reviews

Customer segments

Buyer: district multilingual directors. Users: intake staff, counselors, teachers. Served: newcomers in grades 6–12 and families.

Key resources

Multilingual item bank; native-speaker passages; SLIFE-aligned interview

Channels

Direct to EL directors; DESE SLIFE community; MATSOL; referrals

Cost structure

Content per language; engineering; validation study; training and support; privacy compliance

Revenue streams

District license, about $6k–$30k a year by newcomer count; paid pilots $2k–$5k; training. Families never pay.

All prices are hypotheses to test with district directors. Districts could pay from Title III and local funds; allowability must be confirmed district by district.
