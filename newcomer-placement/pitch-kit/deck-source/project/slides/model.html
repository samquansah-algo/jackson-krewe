Business Model Canvas

Key partners

Pilot districts; community and resettlement nonprofits; bilingual reviewers; MA DESE

Key activities

Map curricula; review evaluations; run diagnostics; train reviewers

Value proposition

Credit for school already done. A counselor-ready recommendation in 48 hours, explained to families in their language.

Relationships

Co-designed pilots; a named reviewer per district

Customer segments

Buyer: district multilingual directors. Users: counselors and registrars. Served: newcomers aged 14–21 and families.

Key resources

Course-equivalency map; reviewer network; diagnostic items

Channels

Direct to directors; nonprofit referrals; counselor networks

Cost structure

Reviewer time (falls as the map grows); AI processing; engineering; privacy compliance

Revenue streams

About $120 per evaluation, or a district license of $8k–$45k a year. Families never pay. Prices are hypotheses.

Appendix. Estimated cost per evaluation is about $37 in year one (mostly reviewer time), falling as the course map grows. All prices are hypotheses to test.
