3 · The solution

Passage: from a phone photo to a credit recommendation in 48 hours

Snap

A family or caseworker photographs the transcript, in any language.

Map

AI reads, translates and maps each course to state requirements.

Check

A short in-school diagnostic fills gaps, or stands in for missing records.

Sign off

A trained reviewer approves. The counselor gets the packet; the family gets a summary in their language.

A recommendation, not a decision: the school's registrar makes the call. Passage never asks about immigration status.
