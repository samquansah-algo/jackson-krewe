3 · The solution

Waypoint: a 45-minute check, run in school

Three parts

Schooling-history interview · 10 min

Years in school, interruptions, last school. Same questions for everyone.

Home-language reading · 10 min

A short passage and questions in the student's first language.

Visual math screener · 20 min

Pictures, numbers and audio in the home language. Adaptive.

Three outputs

Starting-Point Profile

One page for the counselor: evidence and a suggested placement.

Teacher grouping card

Who needs foundational work on day one.

Family summary

A text in the family's language, with a way to reply.

Reuses the English screener schools already give. Staff make the final call. No questions about immigration status, ever.

Say: Waypoint is a 45-minute check run in school: a visual math screener, a home-language reading task, and a schooling-history interview.
