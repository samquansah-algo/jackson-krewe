Full 60-second pitch (about 124 words): Meet Ana: sixteen, nine years of school in Guatemala. She's placed in ninth grade with no credit, because nobody has time to decode her Spanish transcript. Ana isn't rare. In Massachusetts, older newcomers in Springfield are 38 percentage points more likely to start in ninth grade than similar students in Boston. Passage turns a phone photo of a foreign transcript into a counselor-ready credit recommendation in 48 hours. AI maps the record to state requirements, a short in-school diagnostic fills the gaps, and a trained reviewer signs off. We sell to district multilingual directors; students get credit for school they've already done. A teacher taught us records alone mislead, so every record gets a diagnostic.
