# Passage: 60-Second Pitch and Deck Map

**Team:** HaYoung Kong, Samuel Quansah, Martin Sosa, Benjamin Karp
**Solution:** Passage turns a phone photo of a foreign transcript into a counselor-ready credit recommendation in 48 hours. AI reads the record and maps it to state requirements, a short in-school diagnostic fills the gaps, and a trained reviewer signs off. Families get the result in their own language.
**Deck:** <https://claude.ai/artifact/71qxqXwRpMkrgPhjmi3FyL> (private until shared). Slide source files are in [`deck-source/`](deck-source/).

## The pitch (about 124 words, story-led)

> Meet Ana: sixteen, nine years of school in Guatemala. She's placed in ninth grade with no credit, because nobody has time to decode her Spanish transcript. Ana isn't rare. In Massachusetts, older newcomers in Springfield are 38 percentage points more likely to start in ninth grade than similar students in Boston. Passage turns a phone photo of a foreign transcript into a counselor-ready credit recommendation in 48 hours. AI maps the record to state requirements, a short in-school diagnostic fills the gaps, and a trained reviewer signs off. We sell to district multilingual directors; students get credit for school they've already done. A teacher taught us records alone mislead, so every record gets a diagnostic. Next, we'll test whether registrars accept our recommendations.

Ana is a composite student, not a real person. Say so if asked.

## Biggest risk

Many students with limited or interrupted schooling arrive **without** transcripts. The first test (review one district's last 50 newcomer enrollments) tells us how many have usable records. If most don't, the diagnostic becomes the core product.
