## Why the evidence-backed plan is curve-jumping

Today a newcomer's starting point comes either from a credential evaluation built for colleges and employers (official documents, weeks of waiting) or from a case manager's best guess. Paso jumps to a new curve by attaching proof to every recommendation: a course-to-credit mapping, skills-check results and a reviewer's sign-off, so staff can act the same week instead of waiting.

## MVP experience in 5 steps

1. **Upload:** the case manager sends photos of the student's records.
2. **Read:** Paso translates every record and maps each course to a local course and credit.
3. **Check:** the student takes the 30-minute skills check with an adult present.
4. **Review:** a trained reviewer checks the evidence and signs the plan.
5. **Plan delivered:** by day 3 (target), the family gets a one-page plan in their language, at about a 6th-grade reading level, and the school gets a standard letter.

## Not in the MVP

- **Official placement decisions:** schools decide; Paso recommends.
- **AI-only plans:** every plan carries a trained reviewer's sign-off.
- **Document authentication:** Paso records where each document came from; a school or evaluator can authenticate when needed.
- **District sales and student-information-system integration:** districts are phase two, after the nonprofit workflow and demand are validated.
- **A custom app or lesson content:** WhatsApp, forms and documents are enough to test the core job.
